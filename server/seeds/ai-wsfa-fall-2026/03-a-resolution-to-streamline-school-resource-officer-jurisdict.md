# AI Learning Aid: A Resolution to Streamline School Resource Officer Jurisdiction for Educational Success

## Summary
Supports keeping School Resource Officers (SROs) out of routine, non-violent discipline such as dress code, profanity or minor property damage, leaving those matters to administrators and counselors while SROs focus on real safety threats.

## Affirmative arguments
### Police involvement turns school discipline into arrests
Warrant: When officers handle minor misbehavior, students can end up with citations or charges for things once handled in the principal's office.
Impact: A record can push students out of school and into the justice system.

### Discipline falls unequally
Warrant: Black students and students with disabilities are arrested at school at higher rates.
Impact: Limiting SROs to safety threats reduces unequal outcomes.

### Trust between students and staff improves
Warrant: Students are more likely to report real threats when officers are not enforcing dress codes.
Impact: Better relationships can make schools safer.

## Negative arguments
### SROs do reduce some violence
Warrant: Research finds schools with officers have fewer fights.
Impact: Restricting them could weaken safety.

### Minor incidents can escalate quickly
Warrant: A dispute that starts as profanity can become a fight in minutes.
Impact: A rigid rule may stop officers from stepping in early.

### This is a local decision
Warrant: Districts and states already set SRO agreements, and Congress has limited power over school discipline.
Impact: A federal stance ignores local needs.

## Likely questions
### Who decides whether a situation is a safety threat or routine discipline?
- For: aff
Answer: School administrators decide first and call the SRO when there is a real threat.

### What happens when a minor incident turns violent?
- For: aff
Answer: The SRO steps in once there is a threat; the resolution only covers non-violent matters.

### Doesn't research show SROs reduce fights?
- For: aff
Answer: Yes, and the resolution keeps SROs focused on exactly that kind of safety work.

### Why should Congress weigh in on local school discipline?
- For: either
Answer: It is a resolution of support, not a mandate; it sets a national direction.
