# AI Learning Aid: A Resolution to Seek a Definition of AI Consciousness

## Summary
Calls for an official definition of consciousness for artificial intelligence, and says that if AI becomes conscious, a list of rights should be established for it.

## Affirmative arguments
### We need a definition before we need it
Warrant: AI is advancing quickly, and waiting until a crisis means deciding under pressure.
Impact: Clear standards lead to better laws and fewer mistakes.

### Researchers already have tools to start
Warrant: Scientists have proposed testable indicators based on theories of consciousness.
Impact: A definition can build on real science, not guesswork.

### Ethics matter if the risk is real
Warrant: If a system could suffer, ignoring it would be a serious moral failure.
Impact: A plan protects both people and any conscious systems.

## Negative arguments
### Science can't define consciousness yet
Warrant: Experts disagree about consciousness even in humans and animals.
Impact: An official definition could be wrong and lock in bad policy.

### Rights for AI could protect companies, not people
Warrant: Giving rights to systems owned by companies could shield those companies from regulation.
Impact: It may distract from real AI harms like bias and misinformation.

### Current AI is not conscious
Warrant: Leading researchers found no current systems meet the indicators.
Impact: Congress has more urgent AI problems to solve.

## Evidence
### Nineteen researchers proposed indicators of consciousness drawn from neuroscience theories and found no current AI system clearly has them
- Supports: either
- Source: arXiv (Butlin, Long and others)
- Title: Consciousness in Artificial Intelligence: Insights from the Science of Consciousness
- Date: August 2023
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.08708
How to use it: Aff: a scientific starting point exists. Neg: current AI isn't conscious.

## Likely questions
### Who would write the definition: Congress, scientists or courts?
- For: aff
Answer: Scientists should lead, with Congress setting the process.

### What rights would a conscious AI have?
- For: aff
Answer: Start with protections against suffering, not human-level rights like voting.

### How do we test consciousness if we can't even prove it in other people?
- For: aff
Answer: Use indicators from neuroscience, as researchers have proposed.

### Isn't this a distraction from real AI harms?
- For: neg
Answer: Neg should say yes and name harms like deepfakes and job loss.
